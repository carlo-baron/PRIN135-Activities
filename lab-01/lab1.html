Churros

Churros are crispy, golden fried pastries coated in cinnamon sugar. They are delicious served warm with chocolate sauce or caramel for dipping.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • Vegetable oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Boil the water and butter together in a saucepan.
  2. Stir in the flour until a smooth dough forms, then let it cool slightly.
  3. Mix in the egg until the dough is smooth and place it in a piping bag.
  4. Heat the oil and pipe strips of dough into the hot oil. Fry until golden brown.
  5. Drain the churros on paper towels.
  6. Mix the sugar and cinnamon together, then coat the warm churros before serving.
